# Marketing conventions for Japanese websites

Status: created 2026-10-11 from the CTA, trust and seasonal notes in the dashboard skills. Thin on purpose; grows in pass 2 (Consumer Affairs Agency rules, niche palettes and type by industry). Claims and ads law: `../legal/japan.md`. Copy: `../writing/japanese.md`. Design: `../design/japan.md`.

## Conversion path Japanese visitors accept

1. Low-commitment first step: 資料請求, 無料相談, 無料体験, LINE友だち追加, 資料ダウンロード. Hard sells (「今すぐ購入」) lose trust.
2. Proof around the step: 実績 with year, 導入企業, 資格, voices with real names (and 「PR」 if rewarded).
3. What happens next, stated: 「1分で完了」「2営業日以内にご連絡」「しつこい営業はしません」.
4. Several channels side by side: phone with 受付時間, form, LINE. Fixed bottom bar on phones.

## CTA wording

| Western | Japanese that works |
|---|---|
| Sign up now! | まずは無料で資料請求 |
| Buy today! | 詳しく見る / ご購入はこちら |
| Get started free | 無料で試してみる |
| Contact us | お気軽にご相談ください |

Benefit line above the button, action verb on the button.

## Campaign calendar

| When | What moves |
|---|---|
| 1–3月 | 受験, 進学, 新生活, 引越し; 3月 graduation |
| 4月 | New school and fiscal year: schools, B2B, HR peak |
| 5月 GW, 8月 お盆, 年末年始 | Holiday notices; travel and family campaigns |
| 6–7月 | ボーナス, 夏季講習, summer campaigns, open campuses |
| 9月 | Transfers, second-half plans |
| 11–12月 | 年末, ブラックフライデー (growing), お歳暮, year-end greetings |

## Claims: the quick filter

Before any 「No.1」「最安」「限定」「今だけ」「効果」 appears in copy, check `../legal/japan.md`. Paid or requested content is labelled 「広告」/「PR」. Prices include tax.

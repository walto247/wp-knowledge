**Roles (practice built on the data):** LINE = customer relationship (coupons, bookings, reminders) for every age; Instagram = search for restaurants, beauty, clinics, shops, 20s–40s; X = real time, news, campaigns, 20s–30s; TikTok = discovery, under 25 but over 40 % of people in their 40s; Facebook = 30s–50s, B2B, local business owners, events and recruiting; YouTube = trust and explanation at every age.

## Myths (what the platforms say)

| Myth | Official position |
|---|---|
| Shadowbans | Instagram: no shared definition; see **Account Status** for recommendation eligibility. TikTok: content can be "ineligible for the For You feed" with the reason shown and an appeal. X: documented reach limits for rule breaks, labelled and appealable. Limits exist; secret blanket bans are not documented. |
| Best posting time | no platform publishes one; use Insights to see when the account's followers are active |
| 30 hashtags | Instagram (Mosseri): hashtags do not meaningfully change reach, they help classify; Instagram is moving to a 5-hashtag cap. TikTok: #fyp gives no boost; too many hashtags hurt |
| Follower count decides reach | TikTok: not a direct factor. Instagram: new reels go first to a small audience regardless of followers |
| Reels always win | each surface ranks separately; photos and carousels are recommended too; reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to new audiences |
| Reposting viral content grows an account | Instagram recommends reposts and watermarked content less; TikTok makes unoriginal content ineligible |

## Instagram (fact unless marked)

- **Ranking per surface** (Mosseri, 2023): Feed and Reels use your activity, your history with the account and the post's popularity; Reels predicts re-shares, watching to the end, likes; Explore weighs popularity more; Stories closeness.
- **Reels:** new reels go to a small audience first and widen on watch time, retention, shares, likes, comments; up to **3 minutes** are eligible for recommendation; aspect ratio 1.91:1 to 9:16, at least 720 px. **Trial reels** show a reel to non-followers first.
- **Search ("Instagram SEO"):** the typed query matters most, matched against **username, name, bio, captions, hashtags and places**. Put what you do and where in the name and bio; keywords in the caption, not in comments. Long captions unrelated to the content count as low quality.
- **Google can index** public posts and reels of **professional accounts (holder 18+)** posted since 2020: captions now also work as web search text.
- **Alt text** exists (automatic and custom); no official statement that it affects ranking: write it for accessibility (practice).
- **Metrics:** **Views** replaced impressions and plays in 2025 (restate older reports); reach = unique accounts; interactions = likes, comments, saves, shares; follower vs non-follower reach is the split Instagram tells creators to watch.
- **Music:** the licensed library may be unavailable for business accounts; use Meta Sound Collection or own-licensed tracks.

## TikTok (fact unless marked)

- **For You:** interactions (likes, shares, comments, follows, watching to the end), video information (captions, sounds, hashtags) and, least, device and language settings. Follower count and past hits are not direct factors.
- **Not recommended** (For You eligibility standards): unoriginal or reposted content, fake engagement, misinformation, graphic content, regulated goods, creators under 16; repeated breaches make the whole account ineligible for a while.
- **Search:** keep captions and on-screen text relevant; a wall of tiny keywords can be flagged as spam; **Creator Search Insights** shows what people search. Practice: say the keyword, show it on screen, write it in the caption.
- **Business accounts may only use the Commercial Music Library**; other music needs your own licence. Turn on the **content disclosure** toggle for own promotion ("Promotional content") or paid partnerships; undisclosed commercial content can be made ineligible.

## X (fact unless marked)

- The For You algorithm is open source (2023; a Grok-based version published 2026): it predicts likes, replies, reposts, quotes, clicks, dwell time and negatives (mute, block, report), discounts out-of-network posts and repeated authors, and gives new authors a boost. No documented "link penalty"; X may limit flagged external links.
- Reach is limited for rule breaks or low quality, with labels and appeals.

## LINE公式アカウント (fact unless marked)

- **Plans** (税別): コミュニケーション ¥0 / 200 messages; ライト ¥5,000 / 5,000; スタンダード ¥15,000 / 30,000 + extra. **From 2026-10-01 extra messages cost ¥3 each** (¥2.5 above 200,000). Messages = sends × friends; chats, auto-replies and greeting messages are free.
- **Features:** segmented sends (by attributes once the target reach is 100+), step messages, rich menu, 1:1 chat, coupons, shop card, analytics (friends, target reach, blocks, add routes).
- **LINE VOOM ended on 2026-09-30**; VOOM posts are not carried over. The new 「投稿」 feature has been available since 2026-09-16 and shows on the renewed ビジネスプロフィール from 2026-10-21.
- No official block-rate benchmark; "under 30 %" is a rule of thumb (practice).
- Link previews: LINE caches OGP hard; refresh with LINE's Page Poker after changing og tags (practice).
- Friend data, chat content and form answers are personal information once they can identify a person: name LINE in the privacy policy (practice).

## Facebook and Threads (fact)

- Facebook Feed ranks with many prediction models (click, comment, share, time spent); for links, the domain's popularity and expected time on the linked page count; engagement bait is demoted.
- Threads ranks the For You feed by predicted likes, replies, follows and profile clicks; the Following feed is chronological.

## Linking SNS and the website

- **UTM on every profile, rich-menu and message link:** `utm_source=instagram|tiktok|x|line|facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=…`. With `medium=social` the visit lands in GA4's **Organic Social** channel (fact for the rule); without UTMs in-app browsers often show as referral or direct (practice). A separate LINE channel needs a custom channel group (default groups cannot be edited).
- **Link previews:** og:image 1200 × 630 JPEG/PNG (`../seo-geo/README.md`); X, LINE and Facebook show previews, Instagram and TikTok bios do not.
- **Embeds on WordPress:** Meta ended open oEmbed in 2020 and WordPress removed the Instagram/Facebook embed providers; Instagram's oEmbed API needs an app (reports of tokenless access since 2026-06 are unconfirmed: test first). Live embeds load third-party scripts and cookies: prefer a static image + link or a facade, or a feed plugin with a consent option (practice).

## Law for SNS in Japan

- **ステマ規制** (since 2023-10-01): the advertiser is liable. Covered: influencer posts the business asked for or rewarded, and **employees posting as part of their role** (sales, product, managers). Labels 「広告」「PR」「プロモーション」 or a plain sentence, clear in the post; not hidden among hashtags, at the very end, or shown for a moment in a video. Reposting gifted posts on your own pages without the label is also ステマ (2026 order). Details: `../legal/japan.md`.
- **景表法** claims rules apply to posts and ads (No.1, 満足度, 限定).
- **特商法:** selling through SNS messages is 通信販売: the 表記 items must be easy to reach (link the 特商法 page from the bio or rich menu).
- **Clinics** (医療広告等ガイドライン, 事例解説書 6th ed. 2026-03): profile, posts, replies, hashtags and staff accounts with 特定性 and 誘引性 count as advertising; **each post is one ad**: free-treatment posts and before/after photos need treatment, cost, risks and side effects in the same post (or a pinned adjacent post), not "see our website"; no patient testimonials, including reposts.
- **外部送信規律:** applies to covered services (`../legal/japan.md`); list LINE Tag, Meta Pixel and similar tags in the privacy policy anyway (practice).
- **Music:** Instagram business → Sound Collection or own licence; TikTok business → Commercial Music Library only.
- **Faces of pupils, patients, customers:** written consent per platform and period, renewable and withdrawable; group shots or backs when consent is missing (practice).

## Monthly report (practice)

- Instagram: views, reach (follower / non-follower), saves, shares, profile visits, link taps, follower change; per reel views, average watch time, shares.
- TikTok: views, average watch time and completion, shares, saves, followers, search share of traffic, any ineligibility notice.
- X: impressions, engagements, link clicks, follows.
- LINE: friends, target reach, blocks, add routes, messages sent and **cost** (¥3 per extra message since 2026-10), clicks per send, rich-menu clicks, coupon use.
- GA4: Organic Social sessions by source → key events (form, booking, phone tap, LINE add); GA4 will show fewer visits than the platforms' link taps (in-app browsers, consent).
- Note the legal checks done (PR labels, clinic wording, music licences).

# A4 roll-fold leaflet (巻き三つ折り): panels and content

Status: **verified 2026-10-20** against ラクスル, グラフィック, WAVE, プリントネット, プリントモール, ホクトSP, 印刷研究所 and other vendor guides, a 厚生労働省 template and US template guides (`SOURCES.md`, section "Tri-fold"). Panel geometry and reading order are fact; the content plan is practice. General print rules: `production.md`, `design.md`, `law.md`.

## Panel widths and order (fact)

- The panel that folds inside (折り込み面 / 巻き込み面 / 中巻面) is **2–3 mm narrower** (グラフィック: "中央の面より2～3mm短く"). Equal thirds (99/99/99) make the leaflet lift, wrinkle or misfold because of the paper thickness (several vendors). Some vendors use smaller steps (プリントネット 99.5/99.5/98, "1.5 mm以上短く"; US templates about 1/16 in): **always use the chosen vendor's template**.
- **左開き** (the usual choice for horizontal text) and **右開き** (for vertical text) mirror each other (vendor guidance; no statistics on how many use which). Flat sheets, left → right, in mm:

| Variant | Outside sheet (外面) | Inside sheet (中面) |
|---|---|---|
| **左開き** (横書き) | inner flap's outside **97** · back cover **100** · front cover **100** | inside of the cover **100** · centre **100** · inner flap **97** |
| **右開き** (縦書き) | front cover **100** · back cover **100** · inner flap's outside **97** | inner flap **97** · centre **100** · inside of the cover **100** |

- Add 3 mm bleed: each sheet is **303 × 216 mm**. ラクスル's table also lists a different product (an A4-finished leaflet from a larger sheet, 207/210/210): do not mix the two.

## Reading order (fact, vendor and design guides)

1. **Front cover** (closed).
2. Open the cover: the **inside of the cover** and the **inner flap's outside** are seen together (the first open).
3. Open the flap: the **full inside spread** (three panels at once).
4. Close and turn over: the **back cover** (map, contact, QR). The back cover is also seen early by people who pick the leaflet up from the other side.

## Content plan (practice)

| Panel | Job | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Front cover | stop the reader in 3 seconds | who it is for, logo, one strong photo, the title (22–32 pt), at most one proof; **no body text** |
| Inner flap, outside (seen at the first open) | bridge to the inside | the problem (「こんなお悩みありませんか？」 checklist) or the concept, why it matters, a prompt to open (「選ばれる理由を中面で ▶」) |
| Back cover | contact and trust | steps (ご利用の流れ) or a short FAQ, a drawn access map with walking time, hours, company details, phone, QR |
| Inside left | the solution | section heading, reasons 01 and 02 (photo, number, title, short text) |
| Inside centre | proof | reason 03, key numbers or a comparison table (objective criteria only), customer voices or cases |
| Inside right | price and action | tax-inclusive price menu, 2–3 FAQs about price and contract, an action box (offer, QR, phone) |

- **Law** (`law.md`): prices tax-inclusive; comparison tables only with objective, fair criteria; customer voices labelled when rewarded; **clinics: no testimonials and no before/after** (a leaflet handed out is advertising); offers and "limited" claims must be real.
- Inside body text 9–11 pt (more for older readers); see `design.md`.

## Layout rules

- **Text away from folds:** vendors publish **3–5 mm** (プリントモール and ヤマガ印刷 3 mm, アイリィデザイン 5 mm); our default is **8 mm**. Small text, QR codes and faces never on a fold.
- **Photos and colour across folds:** fine **inside** (the three inner panels are seen flat together; a panoramic header or photo ties them); on the **outside**, the cover, back cover and flap are never seen flat together, so keep each panel separate. Dense colour or photos on a fold can crack on heavy paper (Accea, グラフィック from 110 kg; WAVE: avoid on folds or use ≤ 90 kg; or add PP lamination).
- **Fold tolerance:** major vendors publish no figure; one vendor cites 1–2 mm. Design for a small shift.
- **Practice (no source):** give the inner flap's outside its own background colour so it reads as a separate flap at the first open; align headings and boxes on shared horizontal lines across the three inside panels.

## Paper (fact)

- 90–135 kg; **110 kg** coat or matte is the usual choice (WAVE: 110–135 kg most used). Cracking on folds shows from 110 kg; グラフィック folds up to 135 kg and offers scoring before folding (紙割れ防止) for 135–180 kg; WAVE does not fold 180 kg and up (scored and delivered flat).

# Law for printed flyers and pamphlets (Japan)

Status: **verified 2026-10-20** against 国税庁, 消費者庁 (価格表示ガイドライン, No.1 report, ステマ 運用基準), 厚生労働省 医療広告ガイドライン (revised 2026-03-30), 不動産公正取引協議会連合会 (表示規約, 施行規則, 別表), 特定商取引法ガイド, 個人情報保護委員会 通則編, Google Geo Guidelines, 国土地理院 and Supreme Court rulings (`SOURCES.md`). The general rules are in `../legal/japan.md`; this file lists what changes for print. **A checklist, not legal advice.**

## Prices

- **総額表示:** prices shown to consumers on flyers, in shop and in newspaper ads include tax (11,000円 / 11,000円（税込） / 11,000円（税抜価格10,000円）…). B2B prices are not covered. A special display rule applies to food during the 2027-04 to 2029-03 rate cut.
- **二重価格:** a crossed-out "normal price" must have been charged for most of the 8 weeks before the sale, for at least 2 weeks in total, and last within 2 weeks. A future price needs a firm basis. Real estate: the old price must have been published for 2 months or more, within 6 months of the cut, both dates shown.
- **Limited offers** (期間限定, 初回限定, 先着): must be real and not extended or repeated; 消費者庁 issued orders in 2025 (ジャパネット, 創建, ユニットコム). Deliberate misleading ads can be fined directly since 2024-10-01.
- **No.1 and 満足度 claims:** an objective survey and its scope, period and source on the flyer; image surveys are named by 消費者庁 as misleading (2024 report).

## Testimonials

- A flyer is obviously the business's own ad, so it is not ステマ itself; but customer voices the business asked for or rewarded must be labelled 「広告」「PR」 in readable size.

## Clinics (医療広告ガイドライン, revised 2026-03-30)

- Flyers, pamphlets and direct mail count as advertising.
- **No patient testimonials**, no "日本一" or comparisons, no exaggeration.
- **Before/after photos are not allowed on mass-distributed flyers**: the exception (with treatment, cost, risks, side effects) applies only to media the patient asks for (a website, a pamphlet sent on request).
- Self-paid (自由診療) treatments show "保険適用外" and the standard cost (a range is fine).

## Real estate (不動産の表示に関する公正競争規約)

- **Walking time:** 80 m of road distance = 1 minute, any remainder rounded up (signals and slopes ignored). For developments, show the nearest and farthest plots.
- Flyers must show the required items: company name, office address, phone, 宅建 licence number, association membership, **取引態様** (売主 / 代理 / 媒介), property location, access, area, land category and zoning, price (minimum and maximum), validity date, publication date.
- Required notes in **at least 7 pt**; some warnings (e.g. 市街化調整区域) in **16 pt**.
- Banned words without proof: 最高, 格安, 激安, 完全, 絶対, 日本一, 業界一, 当社だけ, 特選, 厳選, 完売. No おとり広告 (a property that does not exist or cannot be bought).

## Mail-order flyers (特定商取引法)

- A flyer or catalogue that takes orders by phone, post or web is 通信販売: price and shipping, payment, delivery, order deadline, **return terms**, seller name, address and phone, extra costs. Some items may be replaced by "sent without delay on request", but never the deadline, return terms, subscription terms or special conditions (`../legal/japan.md`).

## Reply cards and QR forms (個人情報保護法)

- State the **purpose of use** on the card or form page itself (通則編 3-3-4: applies to 応募はがき, surveys, campaign forms), plus third-party sharing and a contact.

## Maps

- **Google Maps:** a small map as supplementary directions in printed promotional material is allowed **up to 5,000 copies**, with attribution and without changing colours; above 5,000 copies, or as the main or creative element, it is not allowed; Street View never in print. For normal flyer runs, **draw a simplified map**.
- **国土地理院 maps:** copying a published GSI map into a distributed leaflet needs **approval (承認申請)** when the map fills half a side or more (折込地図); inside a multi-page booklet, a source line is enough (「電子地形図25000（国土地理院）を加工して作成」).

## Photos and people

- Get a model release for every identifiable person (students: guardian consent); check stock licences for print runs (Adobe Stock standard licence: under 500,000 copies). 肖像権 damages are measured by the model or licence fee (法務省 2026 report).

## Distribution

- **Posting:** entering the shared areas of an apartment building against the manager's posted refusal is trespass (Supreme Court 2008 and 2009). Respect 「チラシお断り」, no entry past auto-locks.
- **Street posting:** no flyers on utility poles or guard rails (屋外広告物条例).
- **新聞折込:** each newspaper or insert agency has its own standards and deadlines (日本新聞協会 has a 折込広告 standard; no public national text): confirm with the 折込会社 (ラクスル: from 3 business days).

# English copywriting

Status: merged 2026-10-11 from `global-web/copy-en` and the WSL `content-en` skill.

## Always

- Clear, direct, active voice. Sentences under about 20 words on average, paragraphs of 3–4 sentences, one idea each. Plain English (Flesch 60–70 as a guide).
- Lead with the answer or the benefit, then the details. Headings say what the section gives ("How much does it cost?", "Three ways we help").
- Match the site's spelling (US or UK) and keep it consistent.
- Numbers and claims specific and sourced ("Used by 120 schools since 2019"), never invented. Digits, not words (7 steps).
- No filler ("world-class", "cutting-edge", "solutions") and no translated-sounding phrases. Explain jargon once.

## Tone by audience

| Audience | Tone |
|---|---|
| Consumers | Warm, conversational |
| Parents / families | Reassuring, clear, concrete |
| B2B | Professional, concise, proof-driven |
| Students / young people | Energetic, peer-to-peer |
| Premium brands | Calm, precise, few words |

## CTAs

✅ Request a free brochure · Book a consultation · See pricing · Get the guide · See how it works
❌ Click here · Submit · Buy now!!

## Lengths (guidance)

| Item | Length |
|---|---|
| Hero H1 / H2 | 5–8 words / 8–15 words |
| Home section | 30–80 words |
| Service page | 500–1,000 words |
| Blog post | 1,000–2,000 words; competitive topics more |
| FAQ answer | 60–120 words |
| Meta description | `../seo-geo/README.md` |

## Readability check

No sentence over 25 words · no paragraph over 150 words · a subheading at least every 300 words · passive voice under 10 % · no three sentences in a row starting with the same word.

## Translating from Japanese

Rewrite for English readers rather than translating word for word: shorter sentences, fewer honorifics, explain Japan-specific terms once ("juku (after-school tutoring)"), convert units and formats. Mark drafts as needing a native check before publishing.
